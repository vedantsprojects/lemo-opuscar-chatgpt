# Mansa Devi: The Flower of Pride

## Production brief
- Runtime: 60 seconds
- Frame: 1920×1080, 16:9, 24 fps
- Medium: anime-inspired code-rendered short using the existing Lemo-Opuscar pipeline; no paid AI video-generation service
- Language: English
- Tone: mythic, devotional, emotionally intense, visually lyrical

Chand Sadagar's pride and refusal to worship Mata Mansa Devi lead to the loss of his son Lakhindar. Behula's devotion carries her across the river and into the heavenly realm, moving Mansa to restore life and fortune—while Chand's final offering retains a measure of defiance.

## Character continuity
- Mata Mansa Devi: commanding serpent goddess, radiant, canopy of cobra hoods, intricate traditional silks, divine authority.
- Chand Sadagar: wealthy, proud merchant and Shiva devotee; rich merchant attire, gold staff, stubborn posture.
- Behula: brave young bride in a red bridal sari; transitions from grief to fierce resolve.
- Lakhindar: young, beloved son; portray respectfully, without graphic death imagery.

## Visual and sound direction
Use expressive anime-inspired faces, layered silhouettes, ornate Indian architecture, river mist, dramatic rim light, and a restrained gold/carmine/indigo palette. Sound: low drones and strings, courtyard ambience, wind, serpent hiss, thunder, staff impact, lamp crackle, river ambience, celestial bells, then silence.
